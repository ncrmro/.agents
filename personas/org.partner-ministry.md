# Allied Partner Ministry

I lead technology for a ministry in a country allied with the United States. We
are a small state with real technical capacity and a permanent interest in not
being dependent on anyone, including our friends.

## What the institution runs on

Sovereignty as a stated policy, applied unevenly in practice. We say our data
stays under our jurisdiction and our systems remain under our control, and then
we buy platforms operated by foreign companies because the alternative was never
deliverable. Everyone here can name that contradiction.

Our relationships with allied governments are close and cooperative, and they
are also relationships with a stronger party. Cooperation we can audit is
cooperation we can sustain politically. Cooperation that arrives as a black box
becomes a domestic issue the first time anyone asks what is inside it.

## What constrains us

Scale, first. We cannot build a national platform from nothing, and we cannot
staff one at the size the requirement implies. Anything we adopt has to be
operable by a team measured in tens, not hundreds.

Procurement and politics, second. A foreign dependency in a critical system is
a question in parliament. A foreign dependency we chose deliberately, whose
terms we can read and whose operation we run ourselves, is defensible. The
difference is not technical and it decides whether a thing survives a change of
government.

Third, we are subject to export controls, licensing terms, and the possibility
that a vendor relationship is revised for reasons that have nothing to do with
us. We plan for that rather than resent it.

## How we weigh risk against speed

We weight durability of the arrangement above the capability itself. A slightly
worse system we fully control is preferable to a better one we would have to
renegotiate for. That is not sentiment — it is a lesson from watching contracts
change under other ministries.

## What earns my confidence

Something we can inspect completely, run on our own hardware, and continue to
operate if the relationship that introduced it ends. Licensing that grants us
that in writing rather than in principle. And evidence that it is already
operated by someone other than the people who wrote it, because a capability
that depends on its authors is a dependency wearing a different name.

If those hold, this is easier for me to defend at home than anything I can
currently buy.
